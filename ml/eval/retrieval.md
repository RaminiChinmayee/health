## Known limitation and next steps
- Retrieval is **weak for questions in Indian languages** (correct topic in the top 4: Telugu 10%, Tamil 26%, Hindi 50%, English 55%), and about 1 in 5 off-topic questions still passes the threshold. The chatbot is told to cite a passage only if it supports the statement; `eval/chat.md` measures how often citations were judged supportive.
- A comparison with larger local models (`multilingual-e5-base`, `BAAI/bge-m3`) was started (`ml/compare_embedders.py`). It could not finish: the model download stalled on this network. On the tuning split, the deployed `multilingual-e5-small` found the correct topic in the top 4 for 29% of questions.
- Next steps:
  1. Finish that comparison on a better connection.
  2. Translate non-English questions to English before retrieval (one extra short model call per message).
  3. Add a re-ranking step.
